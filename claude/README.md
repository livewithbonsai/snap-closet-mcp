# Snap Closet

Your Snap Closet wardrobe in the conversation. The plugin bundles one remote MCP connector,
`https://mcp.snap-closet.com/mcp`; sign in with your Snap Closet account when Claude asks.

## Use it

Ask what you own, search by category, colour, season or occasion, and Claude shows the garments
as cards. Ask for an outfit and Claude suggests looks made only from your own clothes, puts one
on a future day of the app's calendar, or logs what you wore. Ask for a diagnosis to see gaps,
duplicates and new combinations. Share garment photos through an upload link and Claude adds them
to the closet, background removed and tagged as the app does.

## Data

Every request goes to `mcp.snap-closet.com` under your own sign-in; the plugin stores nothing.
Claude receives garment tags and closet records. The images saved with your garments can appear in tool cards; image links are not sent to the model. Outfit photos, try-on images and fitting models are not shared through this connection. Policy:
<https://snap-closet.com/privacy>. Questions: support@snap-closet.com.

Install steps for every client are in the repository README.
